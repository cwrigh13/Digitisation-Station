## SECTION 3 – VHS Digitisation Guide (Blackmagic ATEM Mini Pro ISO + LG Combo Player)

Work through every step physically at the station while checking the guide.

### 3A – Equipment names and descriptions

- [ ] **3A.1** The video switch is correctly named "Blackmagic ATEM Mini Pro ISO" (not RØDECaster Video or any other name)
- [ ] **3A.2** The LG combo player model is correctly identified as 3850R-Z243R
- [ ] **3A.3** The output format is correctly stated as MP4
- [ ] **3A.4** The guide correctly states the MP4 is saved to the patron's USB drive (not to a computer, cloud, etc.)
- [ ] **3A.5** The description of the keypad refers to the ATEM Mini Pro ISO's physical control surface accurately

### 3B – Step-by-step instructions (perform each step physically at the station)

- [ ] **3B.1** **Power/Screen:** Instruction to ensure station is plugged in and screen is switched to HDMI input — does this actually work as described?
- [ ] **3B.2** **CHROME button:** Guide says to press the CHROME button on the underside of the monitor (right-hand side) if screen is not on — is this button actually there, in that location, and does it turn the screen on?
- [ ] **3B.3** **USB connection:** Guide says to plug USB drive into the back of the keypad and look for the bright tape label — is the port actually at the back, is the bright tape actually there, and is the location described accurately?
- [ ] **3B.4** **DISK light green:** Guide says the DISK light turns green when USB is plugged in correctly — does this actually happen?
- [ ] **3B.5** **Insert VHS:** Guide says to insert the VHS tape into the VHS slot on the LG combo player — does the tape insert as described?
- [ ] **3B.6** **Preview/Program/Camera 1:** Guide says PREVIEW, PROGRAM and CAMERA 1 screens will show the video and Channel 1 button lights red — does this actually happen when a tape is inserted and playing?
- [ ] **3B.7** **Channel 1 ON (sound):** Guide says to press ON in Channel 1 on the keypad to turn on sound — is this button correctly identified and does pressing it activate sound as described?
- [ ] **3B.8** **Sound check:** Guide says you will hear sound from the video and see the sound bar moving on screen — does this actually happen when sound is on?
- [ ] **3B.9** **Sound warning:** Guide warns to keep sound on for the entirety of recording or it won't record sound, just video — is this accurate?
- [ ] **3B.10** **Rewind:** Guide instructs patron to rewind to the start of the tape — is the rewind function on the LG player accessible and does it work as implied?
- [ ] **3B.11** **REC button:** Guide says to press the REC button on the keypad — is this button clearly labelled REC and in the location implied by the guide?
- [ ] **3B.12** **DISK light red:** Guide says the DISK light will turn red after pressing REC — does this actually happen?
- [ ] **3B.13** **Press PLAY on VHS:** Guide says to press PLAY on the VHS player after pressing REC — is the order correct (REC before PLAY)? Consider what happens if PLAY is pressed first.
- [ ] **3B.14** **Real-time recording:** Guide says the video records in real time and instructs patron not to fast forward — is this accurate?
- [ ] **3B.15** **Stop recording:** Guide describes how to stop recording (pressing REC again) — does this actually stop recording and finalise the MP4 file?
- [ ] **3B.16** **File output:** After stopping, an MP4 file is written to the hard drive — confirm a file is actually created and that the file format is MP4
- [ ] **3B.17** **File location:** If the guide specifies where on the USB drive the file is saved (folder name, root level, etc.), confirm this is accurate

### 3C – Warnings and callout boxes

- [ ] **3C.1** The warning about keeping sound on throughout recording is present and clearly visible in the guide
- [ ] **3C.2** The warning about not fast-forwarding during recording is present and clearly visible
- [ ] **3C.3** Any other warning callout boxes in the VHS guide are accurate and relevant

### 3D – Supported formats

- [ ] **3D.1** VHS (standard full-size) is listed as supported — confirm the LG player actually accepts standard VHS
- [ ] **3D.2** Any other tape or disc formats listed (e.g., S-VHS, DVD) — confirm the LG player actually accepts each one listed
- [ ] **3D.3** Any formats NOT listed — consider if common formats are missing that patrons might bring (e.g., Betamax, 8mm, Hi8) — these should be noted as NOT supported if the player cannot handle them
